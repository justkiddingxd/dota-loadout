# Five layouts for loadout.nyan.cafe

Each is its own page and script on the library (design/kit.js: HeroViewer + Loadout, the address,
names in the reader's language); none reuses the site's panels. Fonts with Cyrillic only.

1. Dock — the hero is the page; slots in a dock at the bottom showing what they wear; a slot lifts a
   rail of its items; the hero from a palette (Ctrl+K). Onest.
2. Locker — a board of slot tiles beside the hero; a tile drills into its items, back returns; heroes
   are chosen in the same panel. Golos Text, Unbounded.
3. Configurator — step by step: a slot a step, its options in one row, Next to the following slot, a
   summary with the link at the end. Geologica.
4. Catalog — browsing first, as a clothes shop: filters, a large grid, a fitting room that stays in
   view with what is worn. Wix Madefor.
5. Paper doll — labels at the sides with lines to the items on the hero, following him as he moves;
   a label opens its items beside it. Commissioner.

Prototypes of the layout: hero, slots, items, styles, link. Gems, unusual effects, kinetic gems,
couriers, backdrops, effigies, video come over to the one chosen.

## Second round: the Paper doll made usable, the Locker made fuller

6. Character sheet — the slots stand still in two columns at the sides (each on its item's side, in
   order of height); only the lines to the items move. A slot opens a wide drawer of its items.
7. Pointer — click a thing on the hero to change it; hovering him names what is under the cursor; a
   Locker-like panel beside, its rows drawing a line to their items.
8. Locker Pro — the Locker with sets (one click), tiles naming item, set and styles, a slot that
   opens with its worn item large and styles as pictures, and items tried on while hovered.
9. Sets — sets (or a slot's items) on the left, the hero, the outfit on the right with lines.
10. Layers — the outfit as layers that open in place, with a line from the open one to its item.
